Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. About Us

Which Is Cheaper is a simple price comparison tool designed to help shoppers in the United Kingdom compare prices per unit. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we use it.

Data controller. The data controller for this website is the owner and operator of Which Is Cheaper. You can contact us at any time at whichischeaperapp@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information that directly identifies you. The calculator works entirely within your browser — the prices and numbers you enter are never sent to our servers. With your consent, we may use third-party services (see Section 6) that collect anonymous, aggregated data such as basic website usage statistics and information about how ads perform, to help us improve the service and keep it free.

3. How We Use Your Information

Any anonymous data collected is used to: improve website functionality, fix bugs, and understand how visitors interact with our website. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification data to others.

4. Data Security

Since all calculations happen locally on your device, your input data never leaves your browser. We implement appropriate security measures to protect our website and any anonymous data that we may collect.

5. Your Rights

If you are based in the UK or EU, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data that we hold. Since we store almost no personal data, simply clearing your browser cookies will remove any locally stored preferences.

You also have the right to request restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to certain processing. In addition, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) — see ico.org.uk — if you believe our handling of your data has breached data protection law.

6. Third-Party Services

To keep this tool free, we display advertisements and measure website usage through the following third-party services. These services may place cookies or similar technologies on your device only after you have given your consent via the cookie banner on our homepage. You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies and storage for this site.

  • Google AdSense — displays advertisements on our website. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. For details on how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Google Analytics — collects anonymous, aggregated statistics about how visitors use our website (e.g. pages visited, approximate country, device type). No personal identifiers are stored by us.

These third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits. We do not provide any personally identifiable information to them.

Legal basis for processing. Under UK data protection law, we rely on your consent (given via the cookie banner) to place analytics and advertising cookies and to process anonymous usage data. Strictly necessary cookies and similar technologies are used on the basis that they are necessary for the legitimate operation of the website.

We implement this consent using Google Consent Mode v2 — the four Google signals are set to 'denied' by default and only switch to 'granted' after you click Accept. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

International data transfers. Google Analytics and Google AdSense are operated by Google LLC, which may transfer data to the United States. Such transfers are made under the UK-US Data Privacy Framework or other appropriate safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

Data retention. We do not store personal data on our own servers. Google Analytics retains anonymous usage data according to the retention setting in your Google Analytics account (commonly 14 months). Advertising cookies set by Google typically persist for up to 13 months. You can delete all cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings.

Hosting. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company). GitHub may process basic technical data — such as your IP address and browser type — in server logs, solely to provide, maintain and secure the hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

7. Advertising and Your Choices

If you consent to advertising cookies, Google may personalise the ads you see. If you decline, ads will still appear but they will be less relevant to you. You can control or opt out of personalised advertising at any time:

  • Visit Google Ads Settings to manage the ads Google shows you.
  • Visit YourAdChoices or Network Advertising Initiative to opt out of personalised advertising from participating companies.
  • Set your browser to block or delete cookies — see our Cookie Policy for details.
