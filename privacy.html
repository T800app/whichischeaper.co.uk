Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. About Us

Which Is Cheaper is a simple price comparison tool designed to help shoppers in the United Kingdom compare prices per unit. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we use it.

Data controller. The data controller for this website is the owner and operator of Which Is Cheaper. You can contact us at any time at whichischeaperapp@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information that directly identifies you. The calculator works entirely within your browser — the prices, weights, quantities and numbers you enter are never sent to our servers. We do not use web analytics, advertising pixels, fingerprinting, or similar tracking, and we do not ask for names, email addresses, account details, or payment information.

3. How We Use Your Information

The only data kept on your device is functional browser storage: your light/dark appearance preference (wic_dark) and your last three recent comparisons (wic_history, containing the prices, sizes and units you entered). This data never leaves your browser, and you can clear it at any time. We do not sell, trade, or rent any personal data to others, because we hold none on our servers.

4. Data Security

Since all calculations happen locally on your device, your input data never leaves your browser. The site consists of static pages hosted without a visitor database or user accounts, and it runs no tracking scripts.

5. Your Rights

If you are based in the UK or EU, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data that we hold. Since we store almost no personal data on our servers, clearing your browser local storage removes the only locally kept data (your theme preference and recent comparisons).

You also have the right to request restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to certain processing. In addition, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) — see ico.org.uk — if you believe our handling of your data has breached data protection law.

6. Third-Party Services and Hosting

This website does not use Google Analytics, Google AdSense, or any other advertising, analytics, or tracking service, and no visitor data is shared with advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers. No third-party widgets, maps, comment forms, or embeds are loaded.

Hosting. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company). GitHub may process standard technical data — such as your IP address, browser type, and request time — in server logs solely to provide, maintain, and secure the hosting service. GitHub acts as a processor for these hosting logs under its own privacy statement; we do not maintain access to visitor-level server logs.

Legal basis. The only device-side data consists of functional local storage that you control (appearance preference and recent comparisons), kept to provide the features you requested. We run no consent-based advertising or analytics processing.

International data transfers. The site itself does not transfer personal data outside the United Kingdom or the EEA. Hosting-provider logs are handled by GitHub under GitHub's own privacy terms and applicable law.

Data retention. We hold no user accounts, visitor databases, or server-side personal data. Your calculator entries and recent comparisons remain in your browser local storage until you clear them or use "Clear History"; hosting-provider logs follow GitHub's retention policy.

7. No Advertising or Tracking

This website displays no paid advertisements, affiliate banners, or sponsored listings, and it runs no third-party tracking scripts. General product and price information discussed on this site is provided for guidance only and does not constitute an endorsement of any brand, product, or service.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: whichischeaperapp@gmail.com
