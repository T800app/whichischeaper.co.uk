Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

To find out more about cookies, visit allaboutcookies.org — this provides comprehensive information about cookies and how they are used.

How we use cookies

We ask for your consent before placing any non-essential cookies. We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They are usually only set in response to actions made by you which amount to a request for services, such as setting your privacy preferences or filling in forms. You can set your browser to block or alert you about these cookies, but some parts of the site will not then work. These cookies do not store any personally identifiable information.
  • Analytics/performance cookies: With your consent, we use Google Analytics to recognise and count the number of visitors and to see how visitors move around our website. This helps us improve the way our website works. The data collected is anonymous and aggregated.
  • Advertising cookies: With your consent, we use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google uses advertising cookies to show you ads that are more relevant to you, to limit how many times you see an ad, and to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. These cookies track your activity across websites. For details on how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

What happens if you decline cookies?

If you click "Decline" on the cookie banner, we will not place analytics or advertising cookies. The website will still work fully, and you will still see advertisements — but they will be generic and less relevant to you, rather than personalised.

Withdrawing your consent

You can change your mind at any time. Simply clear your browser's cookies and local storage for this website — the cookie banner will appear again on your next visit, and you can make a new choice.

Opting out of personalised advertising

Even if you have consented, you can opt out of personalised advertising at any time:

  • Visit Google Ads Settings to manage or turn off personalised ads from Google.
  • Visit YourAdChoices or Network Advertising Initiative to opt out from participating ad networks.

Managing cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish — for details, see allaboutcookies.org/manage-cookies. You can delete all cookies that are already on your computer, and you can set most browsers to prevent them from being placed. If you do this, however, you may have to manually adjust some preferences every time you visit a site and some services and functionalities may not work properly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our use of cookies.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy, please contact us using the details provided on our homepage.
