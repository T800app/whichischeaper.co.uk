Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

To find out more about cookies, visit allaboutcookies.org — this provides comprehensive information about cookies and how they are used.

Our use of cookies and similar technologies complies with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR), which requires us to obtain your consent before placing non-essential cookies on your device. Essential cookies necessary for the site to function may be set without consent.

What this site uses

This calculator sets no advertising cookies, no analytics or measurement cookies, and no third-party tracking cookies. There are no Google Analytics, Google AdSense, or similar tags on this site. The only data kept on your device is functional browser storage for your own preferences and recent comparisons; it is never sent to our servers.

  • Local storage — theme preference (wic_dark): remembers whether you prefer light or dark mode after you use the theme toggle. It stays on your device and is not used for advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking.
  • Local storage — recent comparisons (wic_history): keeps your last three price comparisons (the prices, sizes and units you typed) so you can reopen them on this device. It contains no account details and can be cleared at any time with the "Clear History" button or your browser settings.
  • Calculator inputs: prices, weights, quantities and other numbers you enter are processed entirely in your browser and are not used for profiling or advertising.

No consent banner

Because the site sets no non-essential (advertising or analytics) cookies or similar tracking technologies, no Accept/Decline consent banner is required under the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), and none is shown. The calculator works fully even if you block or clear browser storage.

No advertising or tracking

This website displays no advertisements and runs no third-party advertising or analytics tags. No data about your visits is shared with advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers.

Managing cookies

You can clear the locally stored preference and history at any time through your browser settings (clear site data / local storage for this site), or use the "Clear History" button in the calculator. Clearing this data only resets the appearance and removes your saved recent comparisons — the calculator and all results work normally. For general information about browser cookies, see allaboutcookies.org/manage-cookies.
